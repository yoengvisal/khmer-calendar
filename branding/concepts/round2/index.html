11

Royal Seal

ត្រាប្រតិទិនខ្មែរ

A seal in the style of a Khmer royal stamp: "ប្រតិទិនខ្មែរ" runs around a red band crowned with kbach flames, with the lunar moon and prasat at the centre. Clearly Khmer and official. Too detailed for small icons, so pair it with a simpler icon.

12

Pagoda Gable

ហោជាងវត្ត

The gable of a Khmer wat roof, with upturned chovea hooks and kbach on the eaves, framing the half-lit lunar moon above a colonnade. Temple architecture, so it reads as Cambodian straight away.

13

Krama Calendar

ក្រមាប្រតិទិន

A calendar page whose header is woven in the red-and-white check of the krama scarf, worn across Cambodia. Below it sit the lunar moon and prasat. Everyday Khmer life plus the calendar.

14

Kbach Kor

ក្បាច់ក

Monogram 09 (ក in the waxing/waning moon) set in a wreath of kbach flame leaves, the motif carved on Khmer temples. Script plus ornament, so it reads as Khmer twice over.

15

Angkor Bands

ប្រាសាទ ពណ៌ជាតិ

National colours (blue, red, blue) with three white prasats in front of the half-lit moon. A nod to the flag without copying it. The most patriotic option; national symbols can have usage rules, so check before shipping it.

16

Naga Moon

នាគព្រះចន្ទ

The seven-headed naga from the Angkor causeway balustrades, its body curving around the half-lit moon and prasat. A powerful Khmer guardian symbol.
