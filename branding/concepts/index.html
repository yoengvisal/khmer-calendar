ប្រតិទិនខ្មែរ · Khmer Calendar10 logo concepts. Pick a number (01–10), or combine ideas from two.

01

Prasat Moon

ព្រះចន្ទប្រាសាទ

A calendar page holding the lunar month: the waxing half (កើត) solid gold, the waning half (រោច) outlined, and a prasat standing on the line between them.

02

Lunar Ring

រង្វង់ខែ

Thirty days around the circle: fifteen gold waxing days grow toward the full moon, fifteen outlined waning days shrink away, with a lotus at the centre.

03

Kbach Crescent

ក្បាច់អឌ្ឍចន្ទ

A crescent moon whose tip curls into a kbach flourish from Khmer ornament, with a star on deep ceremonial red. Calligraphic and warm.

04

Lotus Moon

ឈូកព្រះចន្ទ

A full moon rising out of an opening lotus, linking the Buddhist holy days and the lunar month in one soft, celebratory form.

05

Full-Moon Day

ថ្ងៃ១៥កើត

A tear-off calendar showing ១៥, the full-moon day, in Khmer numerals with a small gold moon. The most literal and readable option. Uses the Kantumruy Pro font.

06

Moon Boat

ទូកព្រះចន្ទ

A Water Festival racing boat with a naga prow under the full moon of Kadeuk, when Bon Om Touk and Sampeah Preah Khae are held. Festive and storytelling.

07

Angkor Horizon

ជើងមេឃអង្គរ

Three prasat towers on the horizon, reflected in the baray at dusk, with the moon overhead. Evokes heritage and place.

08

Phase Grid

តារាងដំណាក់ខែ

Nine moon phases set as a calendar grid, from new moon to full moon and back, with the full-moon holy day ringed in red. Modern and systematic.

09

Kor Monogram

អក្សរ ក

The first Khmer consonant ក (the first letter of កើត, "waxing") set inside a half-lit moon, its colour flipping across the light/dark line. Bold and typographic. Uses the Kantumruy Pro font.

10

Sila Days

ថ្ងៃសីល

A month grid where the four holy days stand out as moons: 8 Kert, 15 Kert, 8 Roch and the new moon. Shows what the app is for at a glance.

Each card shows the app icon at 200, 64, 40 and 24 px on white and black. Concepts 05 and 09 use the Kantumruy Pro font (SIL OFL); the text will be converted to outlines once one is chosen. All artwork is original to this project.
